About Me

I am a Continuous Improvement Engineer with 6+ years of manufacturing experience spanning production leadership, quality, fabrication, process improvement, industrial automation, and software development. I identify operational bottlenecks and independently build practical digital and physical solutions that improve productivity, visibility, quality, and decision-making.

Today I design and deploy custom manufacturing software, AI applications, ERP integrations, mobile systems, automation tools, analytics platforms, and shop-floor technology inside a full-scale sheet metal fabrication operation. That work spans Six Sigma methodologies, Kaizen events, KPI development, Epicor ERP, Python development, REST APIs, SQL databases, AI/LLM deployment, industrial hardware, and cross-functional project leadership.

I got here from the floor. I worked as a Quality Control Inspector performing weld and dimensional inspection, and before that as a Production Coordinator leading two teams—an assembly team of 14 and a powder coat team of 5. Earlier I worked as an Assembly Floater and Assembly Operator / backup Cell Leader in automated production environments supporting pick-and-place robotics and ERS systems. I also bring a creative and technical background from my time as a graphic designer, creating merchandise for artists and coordinating production directly with overseas factories.

That combination is the point: I translate real shop-floor problems into working software and automation, and I build technology around existing manufacturing workflows instead of forcing operations to adapt to generic tools. I am currently completing an Engineering Technology associate degree while working professionally in Continuous Improvement Engineering. My main goal is continuous self-improvement—building my skills, increasing my value, and ultimately improving my family's quality of life through hard work, learning, and long-term growth.
